Zayt w Mouneh brings together mouneh and everyday Lebanese pantry staples, from grains and herbs to honey, olive oil, spices and more.

The selection includes products associated with the Bekaa, Koura, Mount Lebanon and Chouf — roots we show more fully on the About page.
