A note from our pantry

Since 2006, Zayt w Mouneh has kept the Lebanese pantry close to everyday life: grains, herbs, mouneh, honey, oil, spices and the ingredients that make a table feel familiar.

Most of our pantry selection comes from the Bekaa. Our olive oil comes from Koura, our honey from Mount Lebanon, and our debes from the Chouf. We present that provenance plainly, without making claims we cannot verify.
