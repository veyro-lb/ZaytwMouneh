Customer care

Return Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Returns are accepted only when something is wrong with the product or order. Please report the problem within 24 hours of delivery. We will review your request and explain the next steps.

Report a product problemContact us
At a glance
  • Returns are only for damaged, defective, leaking, spoiled, incorrect or missing products.
  • Report the problem within 24 hours of delivery.
  • No returns for a change of mind, personal taste or simply because an item is unopened.
  • For further help or reports after 24 hours, contact us directly. Your statutory rights remain protected.

1. When a return is possible

We accept return or replacement requests only if something is wrong with the product or the order, such as a damaged or leaking item, a genuine quality or safety defect, a spoiled product, or an incorrect or missing item.

We do not accept returns or exchanges just because you changed your mind, no longer need the item, dislike its taste, or want to return an otherwise correct, undamaged product. Being unopened does not, by itself, make a product returnable.

2. Report within 24 hours

Please submit your product-problem request within 24 hours after delivery, using the delivery time as the starting point—not the order or payment time.

If more than 24 hours have passed, or the recorded delivery time is incorrect, please contact us so we can advise you. This reporting procedure does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

3. How to report a problem

Go to Returns & Product Issues, verify your order code and order contact information, then select the affected product, describe what is wrong and submit the request. Add photographs where helpful.

Please keep the item and packaging for review when safe to do so. If you suspect a food-safety problem, stop using or consuming the product; do not keep or transport anything unsafe.

4. Review and possible outcomes

Submitting a request does not automatically approve a return or refund. We review the problem and may ask for photos or more information.

If the issue is confirmed, we will agree on an appropriate solution, which may be a replacement, exchange or a partial or full refund for the affected item(s). A problem with one item does not automatically qualify the entire order for a refund.

5. Refunds and arrangements

Any approved refund is based on the amount actually paid for the affected item(s), accounting for discounts and any previous refunds. We will explain the refund method and timing after reviewing the case.

If collection, return or redelivery is needed, we will confirm the arrangements before asking you to send anything back. For a mistake on our side, we will arrange reasonable corrective steps.

6. More help and consumer rights

For other questions, late reports or anything not covered above, please contact Zayt w Mouneh through our contact page or WhatsApp (+961 70 381 412).

Nothing in this policy removes or limits rights granted by applicable Lebanese consumer-protection law. We will consider any issue that must be addressed under the law even outside our usual 24-hour reporting process.
