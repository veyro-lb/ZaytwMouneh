Customer care

Returns, Exchanges & Product Issues Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Zayt W Mouneh wants customers to be satisfied with every order. If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, missing, or has a genuine quality or safety issue, you can submit a request from the related order for review.

View my orders
At a glance
  • Unopened change-of-mind requests: normally within 10 calendar days of delivery.
  • Visible delivery issues: please report within 48 hours whenever reasonably possible.
  • Quality, spoilage and safety issues remain separately reportable even after opening.
  • A submitted request is reviewed; it is not an automatic refund.

1. Unused and unopened products

Unused and unopened products may be eligible for return or exchange when requested within 10 calendar days of delivery, provided the product remains sealed, unused, in its original condition and original packaging, and is suitable for inspection and return.

The 10-day period is measured from delivery, not from the order date or payment date. If a reliable delivery timestamp is unavailable, the request is sent for manual review rather than using an invented date.

Products that have been opened or used are normally not eligible for a change-of-mind return or exchange. This does not prevent you from reporting a genuine defect, quality issue, safety concern, incorrect product, missing product, or another issue protected under applicable law.

2. Damaged, leaking, missing or incorrect products

For visible delivery issues such as breakage, leakage, missing products or incorrect products, please contact Zayt W Mouneh within 48 hours whenever reasonably possible so the matter can be investigated promptly.

The 48-hour period is a recommended reporting period, not an automatic rejection cutoff. Requests submitted later can still be sent and will be reviewed manually.

We may request reasonable supporting information such as a clear photo of the affected item, packaging, label, batch/lot or expiry details when those details are useful. A photograph of an empty box is not required merely to report a missing item.

3. Product quality, spoilage and safety concerns

A quality, spoilage or safety concern is not treated as an ordinary change-of-mind return. You may report a genuine hidden defect or safety concern even if the product was opened or the ordinary 10-day change-of-mind period has passed.

Do not consume more of a product that you believe may be unsafe merely to prove the issue. We will not require an unsafe or impractical product to be transported or returned solely for proof. Zayt W Mouneh may mark a return as not required for safety reasons.

Where relevant, we may ask when the issue was discovered and request product, packaging, best-before/expiry, batch/lot, storage or use information so we can investigate responsibly.

4. Personal taste and customer-caused issues

Personal taste or preference alone is not considered a product defect when the product is safe, correctly described and conforms to what was purchased. For example, disliking a flavour or deciding after opening that a product is stronger or sweeter than expected does not normally make it defective.

A request may be declined when the available information reasonably establishes that the issue resulted from improper storage, misuse, tampering or customer-caused damage. We review the circumstances rather than automatically accusing a customer of wrongdoing.

5. Please keep the product and packaging

Please keep the affected product and its original packaging until your request has been reviewed, unless keeping the product would be unsafe or impractical. This can help preserve condition, label, batch/lot and expiry information.

6. Review and possible resolutions

Submitting a request does not automatically guarantee a refund or exchange. Each request is reviewed individually. Providing photographs or other evidence also does not automatically guarantee approval.

Depending on the circumstances, an approved resolution may include a replacement, exchange, partial refund, or full refund of the affected item or items. A problem affecting one item does not automatically make the full order refundable.

For a normal change-of-mind return or exchange, delivery or collection costs may be the customer’s responsibility where permitted. Where the error originated from Zayt W Mouneh, such as an incorrect product supplied, we will normally bear reasonable resolution costs.

7. Exchanges and returned food

An exchange is reviewed and authorized before it is carried out. A customer declaration that an item is unopened does not by itself complete the exchange. Returned food is not automatically placed back into sellable inventory; it requires an explicit inspection and inventory decision, with food safety taking priority.

8. Refund processing

Zayt W Mouneh currently records approved refunds through its existing payment and order process. A refund is not marked completed until the real payment or repayment step has actually been completed and recorded by an authorized owner/admin.

Refunds are limited to the amount actually paid for the affected item or quantity, after applicable discounts, promotions and any previous refunds. We do not ask for complete card numbers, CVV codes or online-banking passwords to process a return request.

9. How to submit and follow a request

Open the related order and choose Get Help With This Order. Select the affected product, choose the issue type, provide the relevant details, and attach photos where appropriate. You will receive a non-sequential request reference beginning with ZWM-RR-.

Signed-in customers can monitor request status and customer-visible updates from their account. If we request more information, reply within the existing request instead of opening a duplicate claim.

10. Applicable consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits consumer rights provided under applicable Lebanese law. This policy describes Zayt W Mouneh’s operational process and does not replace or override statutory consumer protections.
